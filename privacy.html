Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains how Vesper Uploader ("the app") handles information. The app is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Vesper YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos to that owner's YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, upload scope). It uses this permission only to upload, title and describe the channel's own videos.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal information of viewers or any other people.
  • It is not offered to the public and is not used to access anyone else's account.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising.

Storage and security

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's private server, so uploads can run on a schedule. It is never published or shared. The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account under Third-party apps with account access.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to data handled by Google.

Children

Vesper videos are made for kids and are marked as made for kids on YouTube. The app itself is not used by children and collects no information from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Vesper YouTube channel.
